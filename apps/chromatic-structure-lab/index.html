Chromatic Structure Lab

Keeps the value structure of an image while moving its hues into a chosen chromatic climate. Color math runs in OKLab on the GPU; the image never leaves this browser.

Role field

Score s mixes lightness and chroma. Pixels above the chosen percentile of s become focus; the rest stay field.

    Color rulePer color in OKLCh. Hue moves toward a lightness dependent ramp, chroma toward the role's chroma, lightness keeps ρ of its original value. Baked into two 33³ LUTs, one per role.rebuilt by: climate, δ, κ, ρ
    Role fieldPer pixel weight w from a percentile band of s, then box blurred three times (≈ Gaussian). Field and focus results are blended along the shorter hue arc.rebuilt by: q, β, σ, smoothing
    Spatial mask and gamutm(x,y) blends source to target in OKLCh. Out of gamut colors lose chroma at constant L and h until they fit sRGB.shader uniforms only
    L′ = ρL + (1−ρ)·Lᵣ(L)   h′ = h ⊕ δ(Hᵣ(L) ⊖ h)   C′ = C + δκ(Cᵣ − C)
    s = (1−β)L + β·min(1, C/0.25)   w = blur(smoothstep(Q(q−σ/2), Q(q+σ/2), s))
